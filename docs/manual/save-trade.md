PokeDaisy can open a game's save file while the game is closed: you can tidy its PC, change your Pokémon, or move Pokémon between two of your games, the way a trade would. It works with FireRed, LeafGreen, Emerald, Ruby and Sapphire saves (and ROM hacks that kept their save layout), in English and the other Western languages, and with HeartGold, SoulSilver, Platinum, Black, White, Black 2 and White 2 saves on the DS (see [On the DS](#on-the-ds)). The 3DS app has it too (see [On the 3DS](#on-the-3ds)).

Everything the editor offers is something the game itself could have: a nature, a level or a move your Pokémon could really have, never a value the game would never make.

## Save editor

Tap `TOOLS` in the library, then `SAVE EDITOR`, and pick a game. Or open a game's menu in the library (long-press it) and pick `SAVE EDITOR` there. The editor has three pages: `POKéMON`, `BAG` and `TRAINER`. Nothing is written until you tap `SAVE`, and `UNDO ALL` drops every change.

With a controller or a keyboard, the D-pad moves an orange cursor (the first press puts it on the Pokémon you picked, or on the open page's button), A presses what it's on and B goes back.

### Your party and boxes

`POKéMON` shows your party beside the PC's boxes. Tap a Pokémon to see it at a glance, then:

- `CHANGE` opens its page (below).
- `MOVE`, then tap where it goes: another box slot, a box on another page, or your party. Moving one into your party takes it out of the PC, and moving a party Pokémon into a box puts it in the PC, the way the game's PC does. Tap a party Pokémon to swap the two.
- `RELEASE` lets a box Pokémon go, after you confirm.

The game's own rules still apply: a Pokémon holding mail stays in your party, your last Pokémon that can battle stays too, and an egg can't be released.

At the top of a box, the magnifier searches every box and your party by name or species (tap a result to go to it), `SORT` puts the box in order by Pokédex number, level or name, and `RENAME` changes the box's name.

### A Pokémon's page

The page has three tabs. Rows you can change have a little arrow at their end.

- `INFO`: its nickname, level, nature, ability, gender, whether it's shiny, its held item and its friendship. You can raise its level, but never below the level it had when you opened the save. Only Pokémon you caught yourself can be renamed, like in the game.
- `STATS`: its base stats, IVs, EVs and the stats they make, with the stat its nature raises or lowers marked. Tap an IV or an EV to change it. IVs go up to 31, and EVs up to 255 for one stat and 510 in all. `MAX IVs` and `CLEAR EVs` do it all at once.
- `MOVES`: its moves and their PP. Tap one to change it, or `ADD MOVE`. Only moves it could have learned by now are offered: by level, TM, HM or a move tutor.

A Pokémon's nature, gender, ability and shininess all come from one hidden number the game picked when you met it. When you change one of them, the editor picks another number that gives what you asked for and keeps the rest as it was.

### Bag

`BAG` shows each pocket. Tap an item to change how many you have or take it out, and `ADD ITEM` lists the items that pocket can hold. Key items and HMs stay as the game gave them.

### Trainer, Pokédex and event tickets

`TRAINER` shows your name, ID and play time. Tap `MONEY` or `COINS` to change them.

The `POKéDEX` window counts what you've seen and caught. `ADD MINE` marks every Pokémon in your party and boxes as caught, `ALL SEEN` marks every Pokémon as seen, and `ALL CAUGHT` marks every Pokémon as caught, even the ones you don't have.

`MISSING` lists every Pokémon this Pokédex hasn't caught yet (the region's until you have the National Pokédex), in the Pokédex's order, and looks through your other games' saves for one of each. A row shows where the best one is (the game, then its box or party) and its level. Tap it to open the trade that brings it, with that Pokémon already picked: you only pick where it lands. For a DS game, a Pokémon in a GBA game's PC comes through [Pal Park](#pal-park). For Black, White, Black 2 and White 2, a Pokémon in a HeartGold, SoulSilver or Platinum PC comes through [Poké Transfer](#transfer-to-black-and-white): tap it to open the transfer with it picked, then pick the other five. A Pokémon that only evolves by trading counts too: one in another save that evolves as it arrives, or one of this save's own that could make a [trade and back](#trade-and-back) (tap it to open the trade with it picked). A row that can't come yet says why, by the games' own rules (no National Pokédex yet, the island part of the story, a Pokémon holding mail). `IN MY SAVES` shows only the ones that can come. Save your changes before you bring one over.

`EVENT TICKETS` lists the tickets Nintendo once handed out at events, the ones that open a ferry to a faraway island. `GET` puts one in your key items pocket, the way the event did. The game's port only takes you there after the Hall of Fame (in FireRed and LeafGreen, once you have the pass for all the Sevii Islands). A ticket you already have, or have already used, isn't offered again.

## On the DS

A Diamond, Pearl, Platinum, HeartGold or SoulSilver save (the US carts of Diamond, Pearl and Platinum, and Renegade Platinum) opens the same way, with the same three pages. On `POKéMON` you get your party and all 18 boxes: move Pokémon between them, search, sort, rename boxes and release, and open a Pokémon's page to change its nickname, nature, ability, gender, shininess, IVs, EVs, level, friendship, moves and held item, with the same rules as above. The moves offered are the ones it could learn by level, TM or HM in your copy of the game (PokeDaisy reads them from it).

A held item works like the game's `GIVE` and `TAKE`: the item comes out of the bag, and the one it held goes back in. So you can only give an item the bag has, and the bag needs room for the one coming back.

`BAG` shows the game's eight pockets, medicine and battle items included, and `ADD ITEM` lists every item your copy of the game puts in that pocket. `TRAINER` has your money and the Pokédex: its counts for the region (Johto or Sinnoh) and the whole National Pokédex, and the same `ADD MINE`, `ALL SEEN` and `ALL CAUGHT`. Until your save has the National Pokédex, marking every Pokémon only marks the region's. These games have no coins or event tickets here, and the play time isn't shown yet. Diamond and Pearl's Pokédex keeps the languages a Pokémon came in for only a few kinds of Pokémon, so a traded Pokémon from another language only shows there for those.

Black, White, Black 2 and White 2 saves open the same way too, in any language. `POKéMON` has your party and all 24 boxes with the same moves, search, sort, renaming and release, and a Pokémon's page as above. In these games the nature is its own, so changing it changes nothing else. A Pokémon with its hidden ability keeps it. `BAG` shows the game's five pockets, where a TM is never used up, so you have one or none. `TRAINER` shows your money (up to 9,999,999), your play time and the Pokédex: Unova's and the National one. They trade with each other (see [Trade between saves](#trade-between-saves)) and get Pokémon from HeartGold, SoulSilver and Platinum through [Poké Transfer](#transfer-to-black-and-white).

## On the 3DS

The 3DS app has the same editor for FireRed, LeafGreen, Emerald, Ruby and Sapphire saves: press **SELECT** in the library until `SAVE EDITOR` and pick a game. Its three pages are tabs along the top of the touch screen (**SELECT** steps through them, or tap one), and the top screen shows the Pokémon, item or trainer under the cursor with the buttons that work there.

- `POKéMON`: **L** and **R** go through your party and the 14 boxes. **A** picks a Pokémon up and puts it down in another place, by the same rules as above. **Y** (or `EDIT`) opens its page: a list where **LEFT** and **RIGHT** change a value step by step (or tap its arrows), and **A** opens a window to pick one (a nature, an item, a move) or to type a number. Names are typed on the 3DS's own keyboard. **L** and **R** on the page go to the next Pokémon. `BOX` renames or sorts the box.
- `BAG`: **L** and **R** go through the pockets, **A** changes how many you have (0 takes it out), and **Y** adds an item.
- `TRAINER`: your money and coins, the Pokédex marks and the event tickets. `MISSING` lists what this Pokédex hasn't caught, each with the other GBA save that has one (in grey: none yet, or one the games won't trade yet). The top screen says where it is and how it comes: a trade, one that evolves as it arrives, or one of yours that a [trade and back](#trade-and-back) evolves. Bring it with `TRADE SAVES`.

**X** saves. A copy of the old save goes to `pokedaisy-backups` first.

`TRADE SAVES` works as above: **L** and **R** reach the party and the boxes on each side, **X** switches sides, **Y** trades (a Pokémon that evolves by trading asks first: `EVOLVE`, or `DON'T` to trade it as it is; one picked alone that the other game would evolve makes a [trade and back](#trade-and-back)), `UNDO ALL` drops the trades you made, and **SELECT** (or `SAVE`) writes both saves together. If you leave or close PokeDaisy with trades or changes not saved, it asks first.

## Trade between saves

Tap `TOOLS` in the library, then `TRADE SAVES`, and pick the game to trade from and then the one to trade with. `BACK` on the second pick lets you choose the first game again, and `BACK` from the trade takes you back to `TOOLS`. (In a game's `SAVE EDITOR`, `TRADE` starts a trade from that game, and `BACK` returns to its boxes.)

Each game shows its party over its boxes. Pick a Pokémon on one side, then a Pokémon or an empty slot on the other, in the party or the PC: a Pokémon for a Pokémon (`TRADE`), or one sent over (`SEND`). An empty party slot puts the Pokémon at the end of that party. `SAVE` writes both saves together.

Diamond, Pearl, Platinum, HeartGold and SoulSilver trade with each other (not with the GBA games). Any Pokémon can go, eggs too, except the Spiky-eared Pichu, and one holding mail can only go to the other game's party. A Pokémon holding an item Platinum doesn't have (some of HeartGold and SoulSilver's newer items) can't go to Platinum, and one holding an item Diamond and Pearl don't have, or in a form only Platinum has (Rotom's appliance forms, Shaymin's Sky Forme), can't go to Diamond or Pearl. What arrives is registered in the Pokédex, as in a real trade.

Black, White, Black 2 and White 2 trade with each other the same way. A Pokémon holding an item Black and White don't have (some of the sequels' newer items) can't go to them. The pair that only evolves when traded for each other evolves when you trade one for the other with `TRADE`, not when one is just sent over.

The games' own trade rules apply. FireRed and LeafGreen only trade with Ruby, Sapphire and Emerald after the island part of their story, a game without its National Pokédex yet only takes Pokémon from its own regional one, and a party has to keep at least one Pokémon that isn't an egg. A Pokémon that evolves when traded evolves when it arrives, as it would with a cable. If you'd rather trade inside the games themselves, see [Link play](link-play.md).

### Trade and back

Some Pokémon only evolve when they're traded. Pick one on its own (nothing on the other side) and, if the other game would evolve it, the button reads `TRADE AND BACK`. It goes to the other game, evolves there as it arrives (using up the item it needed), and comes straight back to the same place in its own game, the way two players trade back and forth. Both games' Pokédexes register it, as two real trades would. The other game needs one free box place for the trip. If the way back isn't allowed (say the evolved Pokémon isn't in the regional Pokédex of a game without its National one), nothing happens at all.

## Pal Park

In Diamond, Pearl, Platinum, HeartGold and SoulSilver, Pal Park brings Pokémon over from a GBA game. PokeDaisy does it with the two saves: tap `TOOLS` in the library, then `PAL PARK`, pick the GBA game (FireRed, LeafGreen, Emerald, Ruby or Sapphire) and then the DS game.

The GBA game's PC is on one side and the DS game's on the other. Tap six Pokémon in the GBA game's boxes (tap one again to put it back), then `MIGRATE`. They leave the GBA game and land in the DS game's PC, in the first free places from the box its PC is on, framed so you can see them. You can bring more sixes, `UNDO ALL` puts everything back, and `SAVE` writes both saves together.

It keeps the DS games' own rules:

- The DS game needs its National Pokédex, and the GBA game has to be in the same language.
- Exactly six go at a time, from the PC boxes, never the party, and the GBA game's PC needs at least six.
- An egg can't go, nor a Pokémon that knows an HM or holds mail or a key item.

Each one arrives the way the real Pal Park makes it: the same Pokémon with its trainer, nature, IVs, EVs, moves (at full PP), ribbons and ball, its friendship back to the start, and "met at Pal Park" today at the level it has now. It counts as caught in the DS game's Pokédex. You don't have to wait a day between two sixes, or catch them in the park: all six are always caught there in the end anyway.

## Transfer to Black and White

Black, White, Black 2 and White 2 take Pokémon from Diamond, Pearl, Platinum, HeartGold and SoulSilver through the Poké Transfer Lab. PokeDaisy does it with the two saves: tap `TOOLS` in the library, then `POKé TRANSFER`, pick the game to send from (`SEND FROM`), then the one to send to (`SEND TO`).

It works like [Pal Park](#pal-park): tap six Pokémon in the first game's boxes, then `TRANSFER`. They land in the first free places of the other game's PC, from the box its PC is on, framed so you can see them. `UNDO ALL` puts everything back, and `SAVE` writes both saves together.

It keeps the Lab's rules:

- The receiving game needs its National Pokédex, and both games have to be in the same language.
- Exactly six go at a time, from the boxes.
- An egg can't go, nor a Pokémon that knows an HM (the sending game's own HMs), nor the Pichu with the spiky ear.
- A held item goes back into the sending game's bag, so each one arrives holding nothing (the bag needs room for them).

Each one arrives the way the Lab makes it: the same Pokémon with its trainer, nature, IVs, EVs, moves (at full PP), ribbons and ball, its friendship back to the start, and "arrived" today at the level it has now. It counts as caught in the receiving game's Pokédex. You don't have to play the catching game on the other side: all six always make it.

## Collection

Tap `TOOLS` in the library, then `COLLECTION`, to see every Pokémon in all your saves in one list: the party and every box of each game the save editor can read. The title counts them, and how many different kinds you have. Each row shows the Pokémon's number, name and level, with the game and the box it's in (or `PARTY`) under it, and a `SHINY` tag on a shiny one.

- Type in the box at the top to find one by its name or its species.
- `ALL GAMES` picks one game to show, `SHINY` shows only shiny ones, and the order button sorts by Pokédex number, by level or by game.

Tap a Pokémon for what you can do with it:

- `OPEN` opens its game in the save editor, with that Pokémon picked.
- `TRADE` asks which game to trade with (one of the same generation), then opens the trade with it picked. 
- `PAL PARK` (a GBA game's Pokémon in a box, when you have Diamond, Pearl, Platinum, HeartGold or SoulSilver) asks which DS game, then opens Pal Park with it picked.

`BACK` from any of them comes back to the collection, read again, so what you changed shows.

## Your saves stay safe

Each save is backed up before it's changed (see [Backups and RESTORE BACKUP](saves.md#backups-and-restore-backup)), and the change is only written if the file is still exactly what PokeDaisy read. The game then starts from the new save rather than resuming, so an old resume can't put the old save back. A save state you made before the change still holds the old save, so don't load one afterwards.

> [!TIP]
> Close the game before you edit its save: the library has to be in front.
